The Kelmarsh wind farm, with six Senvion MM92 turbines totaling 12.3 MW, produced an average of 3.01 MW over 8,174 hours, closely matching the predicted 2.99 MW. Turbine efficiency varied: Kelmarsh_5 had the highest peak power coefficient (0.611), slightly exceeding the theoretical Betz limit of 0.593, suggesting potential anemometer calibration issues. Kelmarsh_6 had the lowest capacity factor at 20.7%, significantly below the fleet average of 25.1%. This data supports the recommendation to prioritize inspection of Kelmarsh_6, as its lower efficiency may indicate issues affecting overall farm performance.
